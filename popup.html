❓ PasteCraft Help & Information

🗑️ Auto-Delete Uncategorized Clips

Purpose: Automatically removes clips that haven't been assigned to a category after a specified time to keep your clipboard organized and prevent clutter.
How it works: Only affects clips in "Uncategorized". Categorized clips are never auto-deleted, ensuring your important organized clips are always preserved.
Time options:
  • Never delete: Keep all clips indefinitely
  • 1 Day: Remove uncategorized clips after 24 hours
  • 1 Week: Remove uncategorized clips after 7 days
  • 1 Month: Remove uncategorized clips after 30 days

⚡ Quick Paste Interface

Purpose: A floating interface that appears on web pages for instant clipboard access without switching tabs.
Theme: Choose between light and dark appearance to match your preference and reduce eye strain.
Auto-hide after paste: Automatically closes the Quick Paste interface after pasting a clip, keeping your screen clean and workflow uninterrupted.
Show timestamps: Displays how long ago each clip was saved (e.g., '2m ago', '1h ago') for better organization and context.
Max clips to display: Controls how many clips appear in the interface (5-50). Fewer clips = faster loading and less visual clutter.

🪄 Magic Button

Purpose: The Magic button provides intelligent clipboard management features that automatically organize and enhance your clipboard experience.
What it does:
  • Smart Categorization: Automatically suggests appropriate categories for new clips based on content analysis
  • Content Enhancement: Formats and cleans up copied text for better readability
  • Duplicate Detection: Identifies and handles duplicate content intelligently
  • Context Awareness: Understands the type of content (code, text, URLs, etc.) and applies appropriate handling
How to use: Click the Magic button (🪄) when saving a new clip to activate intelligent processing. The system will analyze the content and apply appropriate formatting, categorization, and organization automatically.
Benefits: Saves time on manual organization, reduces clutter, and ensures your clipboard stays clean and well-organized with minimal effort.

📱 Main Navigation Tabs

📋 Clips Tab: Your main clipboard management hub where all your saved clips are displayed. This is where you'll see your recent clipboard history, organized by categories, with options to paste, edit, delete, or categorize each clip.
🔍 Search Tab: Powerful search functionality to quickly find specific clips from your entire clipboard history. Search by text content, category names, or keywords. Perfect for finding that important snippet you saved days or weeks ago.
📁 Categories Tab: Organize and manage your clips by creating custom categories. Create folders like "Code Snippets", "Important Notes", "Work Projects", etc. Drag and drop clips between categories or create new ones on the fly.
How to use:
  • Click any tab to switch between different views
  • Each tab has its own specialized tools and functions
  • Your clips remain accessible across all tabs
  • Use the search tab when you need to find something specific
  • Use categories to keep your clips organized and easy to find

📸 Profile Image Creation

Purpose: Create a personalized profile with a custom avatar generated by AI or upload your own photo.
How it works:
  • Step 1 - Upload Photo: Upload your own photo to use as your profile picture
  • Step 2 - AI Remix: Click "Generate AI Avatar" to transform your photo into a creative cartoon-style animal avatar using AI
  • AI Vision Analysis: The AI analyzes your uploaded photo's colors, mood, and features to create a matching avatar
  • Gallery Storage: All generated avatars are saved in the AI Lab Gallery for later use
Where to find it: Click your profile icon in the top navigation, then access the "📸 Profile Picture" section.
Pro Tip: You can generate multiple AI avatars with different styles and save your favorites in the gallery!

🤖 AI Labs

Purpose: Experimental AI-powered tools for content generation, analysis, and creative work.
Features:
  • 🎨 Generator: Create AI-generated images from text descriptions. Describe what you want to see, and the AI creates it instantly
  • 🖼️ Gallery: View and manage all your AI-generated images and avatars. Download, delete, or reuse them anytime
  • 📝 Summary: Paste long text and generate concise summaries. Ask questions about the content and get AI-powered answers
  • 📊 Breakdown: Analyze and break down complex content into simpler parts. Choose breakdown levels (Simple, Moderate, Deep) to understand difficult concepts
How to access: Click the "🤖 AI" tab in the main navigation. Each AI feature has its own sub-tab.
Use cases:
  • Generate custom images for presentations or projects
  • Summarize long articles or documents quickly
  • Break down technical content into easy-to-understand explanations
  • Create unique avatars and visual content

💡 Pro Tips

• Organize clips into categories for better management
• Enable auto-hide to keep your workflow uninterrupted
• Use the search function to quickly find specific clips
• Right-click on clips for additional options like edit, delete, or categorize
