# 🎨 Icons Folder

Place all icon assets here.

## Required Files:
- `icon-16.png` (16x16 pixels)
- `icon-32.png` (32x32 pixels)
- `icon-48.png` (48x48 pixels)
- `icon-128.png` (128x128 pixels)
- `icon-300.png` (300x300 pixels)

## Recommended Source File (Optional):
- `icon-master-1024.png` (1024x1024 pixels) — keep as your master for future resizing/updates

## Generation Instructions:
1. Open Recraft AI (recraft.ai)
2. Use the "Main Logo/Icon" prompt from `../RECRAFT_AI_PROMPTS.md`
3. Generate at 1024x1024 resolution
4. Export as PNG with transparent background
5. Save the 1024x1024 as `icon-master-1024.png` (recommended)
6. Scale down to required sizes (16, 32, 48, 128, 300)

## Quality Check:
- ✅ Transparent background (or consistent solid color)
- ✅ Sharp and clear at all sizes
- ✅ Logo recognizable even at 16x16
- ✅ Colors match brand guidelines
- ✅ No pixelation or artifacts
