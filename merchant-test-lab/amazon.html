Amazon product listing (mock)

Bullet points + backend search term slots

Amazon mock: Amazon splits shopper-facing bullet points from hidden backend search terms — no Etsy-style tag grid or materials chips. Merchant helps paste backend keywords one slot at a time and stage bullet copy without breaking Amazon’s byte limits.

Preset: Amazon — ~30 backend terms × 50 chars; 5 bullet points

Bullet points (key features)

Bullets are shopper-facing selling points on the product page — each ~500 characters max. Lead with benefits, not tag spam.

Backend search terms (individual keyword slots)

Backend terms are hidden search keywords — buyers never see them. Strict byte limits; no commas needed between terms in the real field (mock uses slots for paste testing).
